All from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), released under **CC0** (public domain).
They were resized and recompressed for the web (textures: colour, OpenGL normal,
and AO/roughness/metal packed as `arm.jpg`; HDRIs: downsampled to 512×256).
